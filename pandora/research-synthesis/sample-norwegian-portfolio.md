## 💎 THE HIDDEN GEM: Proportional Debt Reduction Rule (forholdsmessig gjeldreduksjon)

This is what Kimi called **"HUGE and likely missed by the other AIs."**

**The rule:** In Norway's wealth tax, you can't deduct all your debt — only a proportional amount based on how discounted your assets are.

**The mechanics:**
- Primary residence: valued at only **25%** of market value (first 10M NOK)
- Stocks/funds: valued at 80-100% of value
- **Deductible debt = Total debt × (Taxable asset value ÷ Market asset value)**

**Worked example from Kimi's sources:**
- Assets worth 20M market → taxed at 10M (50% discount)
- Debt of 8M → **only 4M deductible**

**What this means for Monkey's mortgage decision:**
If the mortgage is on the primary residence (25% valuation), paying it down doesn't reduce deductible debt one-for-one. The "keep the mortgage for the wealth tax shield" strategy that rich Norwegians use **may not work as well as advertised** when the debt is attached to a heavily discounted asset.

**The 2026 wealth tax numbers (updated):**
- Threshold: **1.9M NOK single** / 3.8M married (not 1.7M as first reported)
- Rate: 1.0% on net 1.9M-21.5M, 1.1% above
- Municipal 0.35% + State 0.65%
